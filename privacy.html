01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how [Company legal name] (“Foresee”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares personal information when you visit our website, request a launch review, or use the Foresee platform (together, the “Services”).

If you have questions, contact us at privacy@foresee.ai.

02Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, work email, company and password.
  • Launch review requests: launch type, launch date, budget range and website.
  • Launch materials: files and content you upload, such as strategy documents, creative, pricing, media plans and inventory data.
  • Communications: messages you send us and feedback you give.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, and actions taken in the Services.
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see our Cookie Policy.

Information from integrations

If you connect third-party services (for example Shopify, Meta or Google Analytics), we receive the data you authorise, such as campaign performance, product and inventory data.

03How we use information

  • To provide the Services, including running stress-tests, generating readiness scores, fix plans and re-tests.
  • To build and improve Launch Memory for your workspace, so later reviews reflect your past launches.
  • To communicate with you about reviews, your account, and service updates.
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Your launch materials are never used to train models that serve other customers unless you explicitly opt in.

04How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who process data on our behalf (hosting, email, analytics, payment), under contracts that limit their use of it.
  • With members of your workspace, according to the permissions you set.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or the public.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you.

05Retention

We keep account information while your account is active. Launch materials and reviews are kept for the life of your workspace so Launch Memory can work. After you close your account, your data is available to export for 90 days and then deleted, except where we must keep it for legal reasons.

06Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise these rights from your account settings or by emailing privacy@foresee.ai. We respond within 30 days.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email.

07International transfers

We may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers out of the EEA and UK.

08Children

The Services are built for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

09Changes to this policy

We will post any changes here and update the date above. If changes are significant, we will notify you by email or in the product before they take effect.
